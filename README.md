## 🌍 Who We Are

**All Hands On Deck** is an international student team bringing together young minds with diverse interests in programming, technology, scientific research, engineering, and creative problem-solving.

We believe that innovation has no borders and that collaboration is one of the most powerful tools for turning ambitious ideas into meaningful projects.

Our team works across different disciplines, combining technical development, scientific exploration, design, and research to address challenging problems.

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to explore innovative solutions, develop meaningful technologies, and contribute to a better future through science, engineering, and collaboration.

We aim to:

- Explore challenging scientific and technological questions.
- Develop innovative software and engineering concepts.
- Participate in international innovation and research competitions.
- Combine creativity with scientific thinking.
- Build projects that inspire future generations.

## 🛰️ Our Featured Project

### MAG-LASER
**Active Shielding & Smart Nanomaterial Hybrid Defense for Deep Space Habitats**

Our featured project explores a conceptual multi-layer radiation-protection architecture for astronauts and deep-space habitats.

The project investigates the combination of:

- Active Magnetic Shielding
- Plasma-Based Technologies
- Advanced Nanomaterials
- Intelligent Energy Management

Developed as part of the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 – Baghdad**.
